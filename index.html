What makes us different

More than just dance classes.

You want a place where your daughter grows — not just as a dancer, but as a person. A place where she feels known, encouraged, and confident in who God made her to be.

At His Image Performing Arts, we train students technically, pour into them spiritually, and build community that feels like family. Because dance should build more than skill — it should build character, confidence, and purpose.

A community that feels like family

Sisterhood is part of the training.

Company dancers rehearse together, study Scripture together, and celebrate every win — big or small — together. The friendships built at the barre carry off the stage, too.

Meet the founder

Hi, I'm Ella.

It is my heart to see each student leave not only as a strong dancer, but as someone who understands their value, their purpose, and the calling on their life.

Dance has been part of my life since I was three years old. Over the years I trained in ballet, contemporary, jazz, and other styles, and I've had the privilege of teaching and investing in young dancers both in the studio and through church and ministry.

His Image was created from a deep desire to offer something more than traditional dance training — a place where technique and faith grow together.
